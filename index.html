Our promise to parents

  • Pay once

    You buy the game in the store and own it. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no unlock walls.

  • No ads, ever

    Not as banners, not as videos, not as rewards. A child's screen is not ad space.

  • No tracking

    No accounts, no analytics, nothing leaves the device. Our games work offline.

  • No reading needed

    Picture buttons and a pointing hand show the way, so the youngest can play alone.

  • Calm by design

    No timers, no streaks, no daily chores and nothing to lose. Stop whenever you like.

  • Two languages

    Every game is in Norwegian and English from day one.

About the studio

Snellingen Studio is a small studio in Norway. We make games we would happily hand to a young child: small, kind and complete, with nothing hidden behind a paywall and nothing pulling a child back for more.
